Undergraduate Engineering Portfolio, 2017-2021

This website provides an overview of my projects and related activities that I have done during my undergraduate program. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, some of my projects shifted to support an online format but nonetheless, were still completed throughout my junior and senior years.

Throughout my academic career, I strengthened my ability to think critically under the pressure of having 10 weeks of instruction without compromising the effort of my work. Because my course load in college exceeded the traditional 12 units, I honed my organizational efforts in order to complete my assignments ahead of time so that I can review the level of detail is up to par with expectations. This strengthened the support I offered in team settings during projects because I was able to keep my team members focused on the task. This allowed us to successfully complete all requirements in a manner to complete multiple revisions to build upon our ideas and schedule a early draft check in with TA's and professors.
