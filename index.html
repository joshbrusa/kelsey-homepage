Undergraduate Engineering Portfolio, 2017-2021

This website provides an overview of my projects and related activities that I have done during my undergraduate program. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, some of my projects shifted to support an online format but nonetheless, were still completed throughout my junior and senior years.

Over my academic career, I strengthened my ability to think critically under the pressure of having 10 weeks of instruction without compromising the effort of my work. Moreover, because my course load in college exceeded the traditional 12 units, I've mastered organizing my assignments to complete them ahead of time to confirm the level of detail is up to par. This helps me in both team and individual environments because I can support my peers to help them stay on task to complete our work on time while also holding myself accountable in the same regard. In the end, I dedicate myself to finishing anything I start while never forgetting to push myself and my creativity beyond expectations. Based on my contribution, I’m aiming to one day make a positive impact in the world.

With my level of experience in the field based on my numerous school projects and contribution in both undergraduate and graduate-level labs, I am looking for an entry-level engineering position.
